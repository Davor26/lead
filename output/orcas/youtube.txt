TITLE
Why Orcas Don't Kill Humans

(alternatives)
The Ocean's Deadliest Predator Refuses to Hunt Us. Why?
Orcas Hunt Great Whites. So Why Not Us?

DESCRIPTION
Orcas hunt great white sharks, drown blue whales and make waves to wash seals off the ice. Nothing in the ocean is safe from them, except, it seems, us. In the wild, across all of recorded history, there is not a single confirmed case of an orca killing a human.

So why not? In this documentary we look at every known encounter between wild orcas and people, the dark exception of captivity, and four explanations: a menu passed down through families, the grandmothers who carry that knowledge, a careful and intelligent animal, and an old Tlingit promise. Then we follow the Iberian orcas spinning sailboat rudders: hundreds of encounters, and no one hurt.
