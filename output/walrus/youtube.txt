Polar Bears vs Walruses: Why the Bear Needs a Rock

DESCRIPTION
The polar bear is the largest predator that lives on land. It can kill a seal with a single blow. So why would it ever need a weapon?

For more than a century, Inuit hunters in Greenland and the Canadian Arctic have described polar bears lifting blocks of ice or stones and throwing them at the heads of walruses. In 2021, polar bear scientists Ian Stirling, Kristin Laidre and Erik Born took those stories seriously.

In this documentary, we look at why a walrus is one of the most dangerous animals a bear can attack: thick skin, deep blubber and tusks up to a metre long. We follow the bears of Wrangel Island and their stampedes, a hungry mother bear testing a herd in Svalbard, a zoo bear named GoGo, walruses sucking clams out of their shells, and the melting sea ice that is pushing tens of thousands of walruses onto crowded beaches.
